## 🎧 Optimizing Performance

To get the best results with your DAW, adjust these settings inside the application:

1. Open the "Options" menu and select "Preferences."
2. Go to the "Audio" tab.
3. Select your "Driver Type." Choose "ASIO" if you use an external sound card.
4. Adjust your "Buffer Size." A lower number reduces latency but requires more processing power. A higher number helps if you hear pops or clicks during playback.
5. Navigate to the "Plug-ins" tab and click "Rescan" to ensure the application recognizes your installed third-party effects and virtual instruments.

## ❓ Troubleshooting Common Issues

*   **Software fails to open:** Restart your computer and ensure no other audio programs are using your sound card.
*   **No sound:** Check the "Output Device" in the audio preferences menu. Make sure the correct speakers are active.
*   **High CPU usage:** Close background applications like web browsers or unused media players. Keep your project simple by "freezing" tracks that consume high resources.
*   **Plugins not showing:** Navigate to the "File Folder" tab in Preferences and verify the custom VST folder path is correct.
